Smart Wallet — Privacy

Last updated: 2026-08-08 · Non-custodial browser wallet

What we do not collect

  • We do not upload your seed phrase or private keys to a Smart Wallet cloud.
  • We do not require a Smart Wallet account for core wallet use.
  • We do not sell your personal information for advertising.

What stays on your device

Addresses, encrypted vault data (when password is on), settings (including an optional Helius/Solana RPC key you paste), and local caches are stored in your browser profile via extension storage.

Network requests

Your browser contacts blockchain RPCs, optional Helius (if you enable it), price APIs (e.g. Jupiter, CoinGecko), WalletConnect/Reown when you use WC, and explorers when you open links. Those parties may see public addresses and normal web request metadata (such as IP address).

Permissions (plain language)

  • Storage — local wallet state
  • Clipboard — copy address; paste-safety checks on Send/Bridge
  • Websites / scripting — inject a standard wallet provider so dApps can connect and request signatures
  • Tabs — open or focus the wallet for approvals and Ledger
  • Alarms — auto-lock
  • Offscreen — local signing helper
  • HID — Ledger hardware over USB
