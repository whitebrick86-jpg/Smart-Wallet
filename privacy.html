Smart Wallet — Privacy

Last updated: 2026-08-19 · Non-custodial browser wallet

Non-custodial design

  • We do not operate a Smart Wallet cloud account that stores your seed phrase or private keys.
  • We do not intentionally transmit seed phrases, private keys, passwords, or decrypted vault contents to Smart Wallet servers.
  • Smart Wallet infrastructure cannot approve or sign transactions for you.
  • We do not sell personal information or use wallet activity for personalized advertising.

RPC routing

Blockchain requests may use built-in public RPC, a Custom RPC endpoint you configure, or developer-managed RPC infrastructure. An authorized operator may choose Managed RPC or Public RPC for reliability, security, maintenance, availability, or capacity. That operator cannot access seed phrases or private wallet keys and cannot approve or sign transactions for you. Disabling Managed RPC does not remotely turn off a Custom RPC you configured.

Device authentication

After you agree, Smart Wallet may silently create a unique non-exportable device-authentication key. The private device-authentication key stays on this device. Only the public key and a derived device identifier are sent to Smart Wallet infrastructure. That identifier is not your seed phrase or wallet private key.

What Managed RPC may process

When Managed RPC is used, Smart Wallet infrastructure may process the selected network, RPC method and parameters, public addresses, transaction hashes, signed transaction data submitted for broadcasting, IP address, timestamps, and security or diagnostic metadata.

What stays on your device

Addresses, encrypted vault data, settings, Custom RPC configuration, caches, dApp permissions, and a non-exportable Managed RPC device-authentication key stay in your browser profile. Seed phrases and private wallet keys are not included in Managed RPC registration.

Network requests

Your browser may contact Managed RPC infrastructure, public or Custom RPC providers, Helius, Jupiter, CoinGecko, LiFi-related services, WalletConnect/Reown, on-ramps, explorers, and dApps you connect. Those parties may see public addresses, request contents, and ordinary web metadata such as IP address.

Automatic blockchain requests are limited to the selected network, except for explicit features such as bridge, swap, authorized dApp requests, transaction confirmation, or an all-network balance view you open.

Permissions (plain language)

  • Storage — encrypted wallet state, settings, and local registration state
  • Clipboard — copy address; paste-safety checks on Send/Bridge
  • Websites / scripting — inject a standard wallet provider on allowlisted dApp hosts
  • Tabs — open or focus the wallet for approvals and Ledger
  • Alarms — auto-lock and bounded maintenance
  • Offscreen — local signing helper
  • HID — Ledger hardware over USB
